This AWS article presents a continuous workflow for detecting abnormal equipment behavior, routing predictions through human review, adding the reviewed examples to the dataset, and retraining the model.

I keep it in this selection because it reflects a recurring concern in my later work: a prediction is only one part of a dependable system. Review, feedback, evidence and the surrounding operating process determine whether the model becomes useful.
