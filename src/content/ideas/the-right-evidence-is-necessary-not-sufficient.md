It is tempting to treat retrieval as the bottleneck. Give the model the right evidence and the rest should follow.

It does not always. The reader can misread a current policy, ignore a revocation, choose an unauthorized tool, or return an invalid action. An oracle that supplies every required source can still fail downstream.

Better retrieval is necessary when the evidence is missing. It cannot repair every mistake made after the evidence arrives.
