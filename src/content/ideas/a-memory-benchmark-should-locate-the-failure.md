---
title: A memory benchmark should locate the failure, not only score the answer
description: One accuracy number cannot distinguish missing evidence from source, time, authorization, interpretation, or action failure.

A wrong action can begin in several places. The evidence may never have been written, retrieval may miss it, the source may fall outside the permitted scope, or the reader may interpret a complete context incorrectly.

Those failures need different repairs. Better retrieval does not fix an authorization mistake. A stronger reader does not recover a source that never reached the context.

A benchmark becomes more useful when it identifies the first broken stage and keeps evidence failure separate from action failure.
